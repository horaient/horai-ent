The Mark

Seven circles, drawn — not placed.

The same shape recurs everywhere the room does: on the door, on the flyer, on the stamp on your wrist. Gold, bone, or flat black — never redrawn, never bent.

It plays quiet by default. Turn the sound on if you want the full mark, the way it opens a set.

Essence

What HORAI is.

HORAI is a cultural club built as a space where artists can be seen without being packaged. It organizes and hosts events and festivals, but its real product is exposure on honest terms — a room, a crowd, and a stage that doesn't sand an artist's edges down.

Rooted locally first — in the scenes, artists, and audiences already here — with the intention of traveling further as the network grows.

Personality.

Minimalist in how it presents itself, timeless in how it's built to last past any single trend cycle, and entertaining in what actually happens inside the room. Artistic and a little underground — closer to a discovery than an announcement.

"HORAI doesn't sell a night out. It opens a door."
